Chinese Cabbage — GDD Requirements by Growth Stage ​

Overview ​

This report surveys GDD required at each major stage of Chinese cabbage (Brassica rapa var. pekinensis)—seedling, rosette, folding, heading, and flowering—from Japanese and international sources. Direct stage-specific GDD is rarely reported; estimates use total GDD, stage duration, optimal/minimum temperatures, and standard calculation methods.

GDD Calculation Basics ​

  • Formula: GDD = Σ (daily mean temperature − 7°C); days below base count as 0.
  • Base temperature 7°C is most common[1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8][9].
  • Total transplant to harvest: ~650–1,000°C·days (varies by system and cultivar)[3][7][9][10][11].

GDD by Growth Stage ​

Seedling (sowing to ~5–6 true leaves) ​

  • ~20 days nursery; optimal 20–25°C (germination 15–30°C)[2][4].
  • Estimate: (20−7)×20 ≈ 260°C·days.
  • Below 13°C for 10+ days increases bolting risk[4][6][12].

Rosette (outer leaf expansion) ​

  • ~15–20 days after seedling; optimal 18–22°C[2][4].
  • Estimate: (20−7)×20 ≈ 260°C·days; cumulative ~520°C·days.

Folding (initial heading) ​

  • Often ~40–45 days after sowing; optimal ~16–17°C[2][4][5].
  • Cumulative ~500°C·days to folding; +100–150°C·days for transition.
  • Heading stops below 4°C[2][4][6].

Heading (head enlargement) ​

  • Optimal mean 15–18°C; ~10–30 days[2][3][4][6].
  • Estimate: (16−7)×25 ≈ 225°C·days; total sowing to harvest ~650–1,000°C·days[3][7][8][9][10][11].

Flowering (bolting) ​

  • Usually avoided in market production; induced by seedling cold (<13°C for 10+ days) plus spring warming[4][6][12].
  • After flower initiation: ~50 days at 15°C → (15−7)×50 = 400°C·days (reference only).

  • Stage transitions are guides; adjust for region, cultivar, and weather.
  • Total crop cycle often 65–90 days and 650–1,000°C·days[3][7][9][10][11].

Practical Significance ​

  • Stage GDD aids growth diagnosis and harvest timing; supports sowing/transplant decisions and bolting avoidance[2][4][5][6].
  • Account for weather variability, cultivar traits, pests, and soil conditions.

Conclusion ​

Although direct stage GDD values are rarely published, estimates from duration, temperature, and cumulative GDD (base 7°C; ~650–1,000°C·days to heading harvest) are useful for field diagnosis and timely management.
