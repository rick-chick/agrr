Spinach — GDD Requirements by Growth Stage ​

Overview ​

This report surveys GDD (Growing Degree Days) for spinach (Spinacia oleracea) across three stages—germination, vegetative growth, and bolting/flowering—from reliable academic and extension sources. Where stage-specific GDD is unavailable, reasonable estimates use days, temperature, and base temperature (Tbase) from guides and research, with calculation assumptions explained.

GDD Basics for Spinach ​

GDD = {(Tmax + Tmin)/2} − Tbase

  • Tbase for spinach: 4.4°C (40°F), consistent in Japanese and international sources[1][2].
  • Optimal germination: 15–20°C (rate declines above this)[3][4][5].
  • Vegetative optimum: 15–20°C after germination; above 20°C, growth slows and bolting risk rises[5][6].

Stage Assumptions for GDD Estimation ​

  • Tbase: 4.4°C
  • Mean temperature: 15°C (conservative optimum per stage); longer duration in cold regions.
  • Days: Germination 10 days; vegetative 35 days (midpoint); ~45 days total to bolting (varies by cultivar and environment).
  • Stage GDD = (mean temperature − Tbase) × days.

Germination GDD ​

  • Optimal temperature: 15–20°C[3][4][5]
  • Duration: 5–14 days; ~10 days typical[3][5][6]
  • Estimate: (15 − 4.4) × 10 ≈ 106 GDD (~100–120 GDD at 15–16°C over 10 days)

Vegetative GDD (Post-germination to Pre-bolting) ​

  • Optimal: 15–20°C; growth stalls above 20°C[5][6]
  • Duration: 28–42 days[6][7]
  • Estimate: (15 − 4.4) × 35 ≈ 373 GDD (~350–470 GDD at 15–17°C over 28–42 days)

Bolting / Flowering ​

  • Triggers: Day length 13+ hours plus temperatures above 20°C[6][8][9]
  • GDD role: Day length is critical; GDD alone is not a direct threshold for bolting.
  • Cumulative to bolting start: ~106 + 373 ≈ 480 GDD (estimate)
  • Sources state bolting cannot be managed by GDD threshold alone[6][8][9].

Calculation Caveats ​

  • Estimates use typical days and temperatures from literature; actual values vary by site, sowing date, and cultivar (early vs. slow-bolting).
  • Bolting is driven more by long days and high temperature than by GDD alone.

Conclusion ​

  • GDD guides germination (~100 GDD) and vegetative/harvest period (~350–470 GDD; ~450 GDD total pre-bolt).
  • Bolting requires day length 13+ hours and temperatures above 20°C—GDD alone cannot control it; adjust sowing date and season.
  • No major guide recommends GDD-only bolting prediction.
  • Use local measured temperatures for best results.
